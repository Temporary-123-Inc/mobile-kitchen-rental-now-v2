EQUIPMENT & LAYOUT

What this option offers

  • Hands-free foot-pump operation
  • Handwashing basin
  • Soap and hand-towel dispensers shown in the published drawing

The source describes station equipment rather than an enclosed handwashing trailer.

Where it fits

Portable handwashing stations give people a dedicated place to clean their hands near food preparation areas, events and work zones. The published Mobile Kitchen Rental Now drawing shows a station rather than an enclosed trailer.

PLAN BEFORE DELIVERY

Check the fit for your site.

  1. Place stations where staff or guests need to wash their hands.
  2. Confirm water replenishment, waste-water collection and consumable restocking.
  3. Check station count, accessibility and servicing frequency.

Confirm with your quote

Tank capacities, hot-water provision and the number of stations per rental are not specified in the drawing. Availability, final equipment and service arrangements are confirmed in your proposal.
