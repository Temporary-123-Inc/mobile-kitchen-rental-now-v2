40ft Combination Mobile Kitchen Rental

The 40ft Combination Mobile Kitchen rental option provides temporary cooking and food-preparation space. Match the cooking line and preparation layout to your menu, meal volume and service schedule, then confirm power, water, drainage and trailer access for the available unit.

Request Mobile Kitchens availability ↗Start a project brief
EQUIPMENT & LAYOUT

What this option offers

  • Commercial ovens, cooktops, grills and refrigerated storage
  • Food preparation workstations
  • Exhaust hoods, fire suppression and climate control

A combined layout can help keep preparation and storage close to the cooking line.

Where it fits

A temporary commercial kitchen supports meal preparation while a permanent kitchen is renovated or unavailable. Plan receiving, preparation, cooking and service as a connected workflow.

PLAN BEFORE DELIVERY

Check the fit for your site.

  1. List the appliances your menu requires, including any fryers or steamers.
  2. Share meals per service and the number of staff working at once.
  3. Confirm electrical service, fuel, potable water, drainage and the delivery footprint.

Confirm with your quote

Appliance quantities, fuel type, electrical load and meal output are not specified in the model listing. Availability, final equipment and service arrangements are confirmed in your proposal.
